# Privacy

Caravan has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your rides, quarters, goods, gold and buildings stay on your phone, in the app's own storage (one file). Android's backup may keep a copy, as with any app, if you turned it on.

**Location** is used to know which quarter you are in and which quarters a ride passes through. It is read only while the app is open and during a ride, when a notification is on screen. Caravan does not ask for location «all the time». Routes are never sent anywhere; a ride keeps only its distance and the names of the quarters.

**What goes online, and only that:**
- map tiles from OpenStreetMap, for the part of the map on screen;
- the named districts around you and what is in a quarter (parks, water, shops, stations…) from the OpenStreetMap Overpass service, for the point of a quarter's centre;
- the weather at the end of a ride from Open-Meteo, for your position rounded to about 100 m.

None of these requests carries anything about you.

**Bluetooth** is used only if you choose your car in the settings: Caravan then notices when the phone connects to that one device, to offer a ride, and when it disconnects, to end it. It doesn't scan for devices or connect to anything.

**The voice** for new quarters, if you turn it on, uses the phone's own text-to-speech. Notifications are local; nothing is sent to show them.
